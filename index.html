About us

Started by students who wanted to give back.

In 2003, a small group of Western students asked a simple question: how could university students help younger kids with school? Their answer was free tutoring.

We believe every child should have the same chance to do well in school, whatever their family's financial situation. Our tutors come from every faculty at Western. They bring patience, flexibility and a personal approach to each student.

Students leave sessions with a better handle on their coursework and study habits they can carry through the rest of their education.

Programs

Two ways to get tutoring

Both programs are free. Tutors follow each student's needs, so sessions feel relaxed and less stressful than a classroom.

At school

Off-campus tutoring

Similar to an after-school homework club. A group of tutors partners virtually with elementary schools to help students with their work.

Where
Online, over Zoom or Microsoft Teams
How often
Once a week, for one hour

At Western

On-campus tutoring

One-on-one sessions on Western's campus, with a tutor who works at your child's pace.

Where
D.B. Weldon Library or the Allyn and Betty Taylor Library
Format
One student, one tutor
Schedule
Arranged around the student's needs

We cover parking and transportation costs for parents bringing their child to campus.

Tutor with Hope for Relief

You don't need to be an expert. You need patience, flexibility and a couple of hours a week. Many tutors say it became a big part of their time at Western, and a good way to make close friends.
